How to play

  1. Read. Study the sentence for as long as you need. Press Enter when ready.
  2. Recall. When it disappears, type it from memory.
  3. Submit. Press Enter or the button. A timeout submits automatically.
  4. Repeat. Finish five rounds to earn your final grade.

What counts?

Capitalization, punctuation, and internal spaces count. Leading and trailing whitespace is ignored. Missing, extra, and incorrect characters reduce accuracy.

How grading works

Each round combines accuracy and WPM. Your final grade is based on your average accuracy (75%) and average typing speed (25%). Answers below 30% accuracy score zero.

Keep it fair

Paste and drag-and-drop are blocked. The typing timer keeps running when you switch tabs. Personal bests are stored in this browser, separately for each difficulty.
