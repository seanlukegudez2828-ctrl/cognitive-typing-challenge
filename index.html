How to play

  1. Memorize. Read the sentence while the first timer runs.
  2. Recall. When it disappears, type it from memory.
  3. Submit. Press Enter or use the button. A timeout submits automatically.
  4. Repeat. Finish five rounds to get your session score.

What counts?

Capitalization, punctuation, and internal spaces count. Leading and trailing whitespace is ignored. Missing, extra, and incorrect characters reduce accuracy.

How scoring works

WPM uses credited characters and typing time. Your score combines accuracy and WPM. Answers below 30% accuracy score zero. A one-second minimum is used when calculating WPM.

Keep it fair

Paste and drag-and-drop are blocked. Timers keep running when you switch tabs. Personal bests are stored in this browser, separately for each difficulty.
